About yt-analytics-private

Terms of use

Effective October 24, 2026. Operator: Gui Latrova.

Personal use

yt-analytics-private is a personal, read-only reporting tool for YouTube channels owned or legitimately controlled by its operator. It is not a public subscription service and offers no public account registration.

Authorization

Only the channel owner may authorize access. Access can be withdrawn through Google Account connections. Use of YouTube remains subject to YouTube's Terms of Service.

Reports and availability

Reports depend on Google's API availability, processing delays, privacy thresholds, and revisions. Numbers may differ from realtime YouTube Studio displays. Reports are provided for informational analysis; the tool does not guarantee complete data, uninterrupted collection, or any channel growth outcome.
