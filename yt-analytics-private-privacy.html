About yt-analytics-private

Privacy policy

Effective October 24, 2026. Operator: Gui Latrova.

Data accessed and purpose

This personal app accesses only channels the owner authorizes. It reads channel and video identifiers, titles, publication dates, durations, privacy status, impressions, click-through rates, views, watch time, engagement counts, subscriber gains and losses, aggregate traffic sources, and audience retention. It uses these reports for the owner's channel performance analysis.

The app does not request passwords, revenue data, or write permissions. Google handles sign-in and consent. The app stores OAuth credentials so the owner's scheduled collector can refresh access without signing in every day.

Storage and access

OAuth client credentials and channel tokens are stored in a restricted directory on the owner's computer. They are not included in source control or public reports. Collected metrics are saved in the owner's local project files and may be reviewed by the owner and his authorized software agents. The owner controls whether collected report files are copied or committed to a repository.

The app does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to data brokers. Data is used only for the channel analysis described here. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

Report files are retained until the owner deletes them. Removing a channel from the collector deletes its local authorization; existing reports remain until separately deleted. The owner can revoke the Google grant in Google Account connections and delete local reports and any repository or backup copies under his control.

This public website

These information pages do not offer Google sign-in or receive OAuth callbacks. They contain no analytics scripts or tracking cookies added by this app. The hosting provider, Vercel, may process ordinary request information to deliver and secure the website; see Vercel's privacy policy.
