yt-analytics-private

A personal tool operated by Gui Latrova to collect daily performance reports for his own YouTube channels.

What the app does

After the owner authorizes a channel, the app reads upload metadata, thumbnail impressions and click-through rates, views, watch time, engagement, subscriber changes, traffic sources, and audience retention. The reports help the owner understand channel growth and improve future videos.

The app requests only read-only YouTube and YouTube Analytics permissions. It does not upload, edit, or delete videos, publish comments, or access revenue reports. There is no public registration or sign-in on this website.

Access and control

Each channel is authorized separately through Google. The owner can stop collection and revoke access at any time in Google Account connections.
